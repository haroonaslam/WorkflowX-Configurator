# Natural authoring

Use connected prose with the same level of visual detail as JSON. Open the pose description with a sentence that establishes the overall stance, orientation, and support. Then give the torso, hands, legs, and feet distinct clauses or sentences when their positions or contact points matter. Identify the relevant anatomical side and clarify its location in the frame where useful. Weave visibility into the sentence that describes the relevant pose, object, or overlap. Bring camera placement, framing, focus, and relevant photographic settings together in natural language. For edits, establish reference roles first, then describe the target image, what changes, and what stays.

When the final exclusion check calls for one, end the positive prompt with one short, scene-specific exclusion sentence. Otherwise end with the desired visual description.
