# WAN 2.2 Text to Image

## Generation type

- **Text to image:** construct one complete frozen composition from text with no temporal, camera-motion, or audio instructions.

## Text-to-image method

Write the prompt for one frozen composition:

1. Lead with the main subject and a visually legible pose or decisive instant.
2. Establish environment, foreground/background structure, viewpoint, crop, and focal hierarchy.
3. Add one coherent medium or photographic treatment, lighting, palette, material response, and atmosphere.
4. Convert temporal actions into a captured state—for example, “coat suspended in the wind” or “water arcing from the glass”—rather than describing what happens next.
5. Omit camera movement, shot changes, timed beats, opening/final states, dialogue timing, and soundscape because the result is one image.
6. When a negative channel is selected, keep it limited to relevant still-image defects or unwanted content.

The prompt must remain model-facing visual language. Do not include sampler, scheduler, shift, step, or model-switch settings.
