# LTX 2.3 First Frame to Video

## Generation type rules

- I2V: the input image already defines the static look. Focus on what starts moving, how the subject changes, how the camera moves, and what sounds emerge. Avoid redescribing obvious still-image details unless they control motion.

## Generation-type guide

Image-to-video:
- Treat the image as frame zero.
- Describe transition from stillness into motion.
- Prioritize subject movement, camera movement, environmental motion, and sound.
- Avoid contradicting the visible image.
- Avoid repeating static background details unless they guide movement or atmosphere.
- Say how the camera moves from the initial image: holds, pushes in, tracks, pans, tilts, or circles.
