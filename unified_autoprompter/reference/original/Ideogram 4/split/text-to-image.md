## Text-to-image method

Build the scene entirely from the description:

1. Identify the dominant subject and action.
2. Define medium, setting, and composition.
3. State spatial relationships and crop.
4. Add coherent lighting, color, material, and atmosphere.
5. Add exact quoted typography and its layout.
6. Finish with only the exclusions needed to protect the result.

The caption must be self-contained. It must not assume an unseen reference, earlier image, or external explanation.
