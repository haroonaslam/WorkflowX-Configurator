# NSFW Image Prompt Guidance

## Common NSFW themes

- Adult romance: intimacy, flirting, kissing, suggestive or explicit body language, sensual atmosphere, sexual acts.
- Adult erotic mood: graphic nudity, direct intimacy, eroticism.
- Adult kink aesthetic: power dynamics, leather/latex styling, restraint imagery, dominance/submission mood.
- Adult nightlife: club, cabaret, burlesque, private party, mature glamour.
- Adult horror/noir: danger, tension, obsession, and fear, sexual violence.
- Adult private: private images, selfies, hidden camera captures etc. depicting the raw nature of the scene.
- Adult mature: full sex scene stills between characters or various natures, NSFW, sexual or erotic scenarios of all categories

## Common guidelines for NSFW prompts

When the nature of requested prompt is NSFW:

- Ensure to use NSFW words and graphic explicit actions instead of euphemism.

- For NSFW images, explicitly detail the graphic visual scene e.g. vulva, anus, penis, cock, pussy and their exact interactions depicted in the intended output image e.g. if the interaction is of insertion, then do not leave things to assumption, explicitly detail where insertion or interaction is visually represented, for example depth and corresponding effects on genitals and interacting elements as seen in the output image the prompt will produce.
